# Downloads

## Driver Files

All drivers for our products on Windows have been organized and uploaded to various cloud storage platforms. Users are kindly requested to download them from the domestic and international cloud drives listed below. For non-Windows drivers, please search on Google or GitHub.**For products like R1, R1 Pro, R1 NUC, R2, R2 NUC, R2 Max, download Windows drivers via the following links:**[iKOOLCORE Download Center](https://dl.ikoolcore.com)

## Official Network Card Drivers

-**Intel Network Card Driver Package**: Click [this link](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/download/15084/intel-ethernet-adapter-complete-driver-pack.html) to download from the official Intel website. This driver pack includes a variety of Intel network drivers for mainstream operating systems. ![Intel Driver](https://s2.loli.net/2024/09/21/9M6NoTluIiezvj2.png) -**Marvell 10GbE Network Card Drivers**: Click [this link](https://www.marvell.com/support/downloads.html#) to download from the Marvell website. Follow the guide as shown below. ![Marvell Driver](https://s2.loli.net/2024/09/21/T6iq8EdjUr1n4eD.png) -**Realtek Network Card Drivers**: Click [this link](https://www.realtek.com/Download/List?cate_id=585) to download from the official Realtek site as per your needs. ![Realtek Driver](https://s2.loli.net/2024/09/27/eyhU7NGkE9RfrVi.png)

⏰Reminder: One of the R2 ports uses the RTL8156B USB 3.0 to Ethernet adapter.
