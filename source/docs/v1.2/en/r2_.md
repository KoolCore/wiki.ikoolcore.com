## FAQ

Q: How do I access the BIOS?\*\*

> A: After powering on the device, press `F2` within 0.5 seconds to enter the BIOS. To access the boot menu, press `F12` within 0.5 seconds.

Q: How do I enable auto power-on after power loss?

> A: Press `F2` within 0.5 seconds of powering on to enter the BIOS. Navigate to `Chipset` > `PCH-IO Configuration` > `State After G3`. Set it to `S0 State` for auto power-on or `S5 State` for manual power-on.

Q: The original power adapter uses a USB-C DC protocol. Can I use a PD charger to power the device?

> A:**Yes**, you can use a PD charger with a USB-C to USB-C cable and a PD charger rated for 20V 3.25A 65W output. In testing, PD chargers can negotiate to either 12V or 20V; most ANKER PD chargers support this. Some multi-port PD chargers might not negotiate to 20V, so we recommend using a single-port 65W PD charger. Other brands of chargers may also work, but customers should test them.**Not all PD chargers can negotiate the required voltage for booting.**> Q: Can the memory or storage be upgraded? A: No, the memory is soldered and cannot be upgraded. Laptop memory sticks are longer than the device itself, so they cannot be installed. However, the storage can be upgraded or replaced. The device uses 2242-sized SSDs but can accommodate 2230 SSDs with an adapter card.

Q: Can the device be pre-installed with an operating system?

> A: Starting with the R2 model, iKOOLCORE does not pre-install any operating system, regardless of whether the configuration includes a hard drive or not.

Q: I encountered an issue where the USB port doesn’t support my solid-state USB drive. How can I fix this?

> A: If the device cannot recognize your solid-state USB drive, it’s likely because the drive requires a voltage different from the standard**(5V)**provided by the USB port, making it incompatible with the R1. Unfortunately, there is no solution at the moment. Please use a standard USB drive instead. Additionally, some USB hubs require higher currents, which may cause the device to shut down when multiple devices are connected. Switching to a higher-wattage PD charger might resolve this. In most cases, USB recognition issues are caused by voltage and current incompatibilities.

> Q: Does the R2 support 2242-sized SATA (NGFF) SSDs? A: Yes, it does.

Q: How do I adjust or disable the thermal limit?

> A: Press `F2` within 0.5 seconds of powering on to enter the BIOS. Then go to `Advanced` > `Thermal Configuration` > `CPU Thermal Configuration` > `Tcc Activation Offset`. The default value is 30, meaning the thermal limit is 105°C - 30°C = 75°C. Users can adjust this value. Setting it to 0 means the thermal limit is 105°C (effectively no limit). We recommend setting it to 15, which results in a thermal limit of 105°C - 15°C = 90°C. The R2 model has no thermal limit pre-set.**iKOOLCORE’s feedback on high-temperature stress tests:**In stress tests under an ambient temperature of 65°C, the R1 model’s processor reached over 90°C without crashing and successfully passed the test after throttling down.
