## Hardware Design

> From concept to reality, we hold exclusive design patents. With the intense competition in today's market, you might encounter similar-looking products online—please be discerning.

- **CPU:** Alder Lake-N N95, i3-N300
- **Memory:** Samsung LPDDR5, onboard, 8GB/16GB (non-upgradable)
- **Networking:** 3 x Intel i226-V (PCIe), 1 x Realtek RTL8156BG (USB 3.0)
- **Wi-Fi:** Reserved E-Key slot for a Wi-Fi card; the default configuration includes a built-in patch antenna but no wireless card module

![R2 nameplate specifications](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0696/1555/0751/files/Nameplate_Specifications.webp)

- **Storage:** 1 x M.2 2242, NVMe/SATA auto-adaptive; NVMe supports PCIe 3.0 x2 speeds
- **Display:** 1 x HDMI 2.0 (4K@60Hz), 1 x USB-C eDP 1.4a (4K@144Hz)
- **USB Ports:** 2 x USB-A 3.1 Gen2 (5Gbps), 1 x USB-C 3.2 Gen1 (10Gbps)
- **Audio:** 1 x USB-C audio output
- **Cooling:** All-copper heatsink with a PWM-controlled, ultra-quiet, dual-ball-bearing fan (maximum speed: 3000 RPM)

![R2 fan design](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0696/1555/0751/files/Fan_Design-Compressed.webp)

- **Compatible OS:** Windows, Linux and derivatives (Debian, CentOS, Ubuntu, etc.), pfSense, OPNsense, OpenWrt, VyOS, iKuai, Proxmox VE, VMware ESXi, Unraid, TrueNAS, and XCP-ng
- **Power Supply:** 12V 4A 48W custom DC adapter; PD protocol chargers are supported
- **Dimensions:** 7.5 x 7.5 x 5.2 cm
- **Weight:** Main unit: 420g; packaged product: 850g
- **Package Contents:** 1 x R2 main unit, 1 x 12V 4A 48W power adapter, 1 x VESA mount, 1 x user manual, 1 x screw kit, 1 x certificate of conformity, and 1 x moisture absorber

> **Special Note:** The device's 12V DC port is custom-designed. Do not use this adapter with other devices to avoid damage.

## FAQ

### How do I access the BIOS?

After powering on the device, press `F2` within 0.5 seconds to enter BIOS. To access the boot menu, press `F12` within 0.5 seconds.

### How do I enable auto power-on after power loss?

Press `F2` within 0.5 seconds of powering on to enter BIOS. Navigate to `Chipset` → `PCH-IO Configuration` → `State After G3`. Set it to `S0 State` for auto power-on or `S5 State` for manual power-on.

### Can I use a PD charger to power the device?

Yes. Use a PD charger with a USB-C to USB-C cable rated for 20V 3.25A 65W output. In testing, PD chargers negotiated either 12V or 20V; most ANKER PD chargers support this. Some multi-port PD chargers might not negotiate to 20V, so a single-port 65W PD charger is recommended. Other brands may also work, but should be tested. Not all PD chargers can negotiate the required voltage for booting.

### Can the memory or storage be upgraded?

The memory is soldered and cannot be upgraded. Laptop memory modules are longer than the device itself, so they cannot be installed. The storage can be upgraded or replaced. The device uses 2242-sized SSDs but can accommodate 2230 SSDs with an adapter card.

### Can the device be pre-installed with an operating system?

Starting with the R2 model, iKOOLCORE does not pre-install any operating system, regardless of whether the configuration includes a hard drive.

### Why is a solid-state USB drive not recognized?

If the device cannot recognize a solid-state USB drive, the drive may require a voltage different from the standard 5V supplied by the USB port, making it incompatible. There is currently no solution. Use a standard USB drive instead. Some USB hubs also require higher current and may cause the device to shut down when multiple devices are connected. A higher-wattage PD charger may resolve this. In most cases, USB recognition issues are caused by voltage and current incompatibilities.

### Does the R2 support 2242-sized SATA (NGFF) SSDs?

Yes.

### How do I adjust or disable the thermal limit?

Press `F2` within 0.5 seconds of powering on to enter BIOS. Then go to `Advanced` → `Thermal Configuration` → `CPU Thermal Configuration` → `Tcc Activation Offset`. The default value is 30, meaning the thermal limit is 105°C − 30°C = 75°C. Adjusting it to 0 means the thermal limit is 105°C, effectively disabling the limit. Setting it to 15 results in a thermal limit of 105°C − 15°C = 90°C. The R2 model has no thermal limit pre-set.

> **iKOOLCORE stress-test note:** In stress tests at an ambient temperature of 65°C, the R1 processor reached over 90°C without crashing and successfully passed the test after throttling down.
